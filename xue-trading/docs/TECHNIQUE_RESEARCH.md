# Technique research — reputation notes (for the learning priors)

Honest summary of what public sources say about each technique's real-world use,
used only to set the **starting** scores. The live results on your account are
what actually move the scores.

> ⚠️ Reality check: headline "win rate" claims on YouTube/blogs (e.g. "RSI 91%",
> "MACD+RSI 73%") are usually cherry-picked backtests or marketing and do **not**
> predict live profit. No technique is universally best; execution, risk and
> market regime matter more than the technique name.

## For XAUUSD (gold) specifically
The **SMC / ICT** family (order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps,
market structure) has by far the largest active-trader following and the most
gold-specific material. Classic indicators (RSI, MACD, EMA) remain popular but
are widely described as lagging.
